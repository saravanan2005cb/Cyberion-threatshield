CYBERION THREATSHIELD - INCIDENT RESPONSE PLAYBOOK
==================================================
SCENARIO: Credential Compromise & Lateral Movement

1. TRIGGER CONDITIONS:
- Multiple failed login attempts followed by a successful login from an unusual external IP.
- Sigma rule alert triggered for credential dumping or brute-force (Event ID 4625 / T1110).

2. INVESTIGATION STEPS:
- Identify the compromised user account and impacted endpoints.
- Review Windows Security logs (Event ID 4624, 4672) to track lateral movement.
- Check for unusual process executions (e.g., PsExec, WinRM) originating from the account.

3. CONTAINMENT STEPS:
- Immediately disable or reset the compromised user account password.
- Isolate the affected endpoints from the corporate network to prevent spreading.

4. ERADICATION & RECOVERY:
- Terminate malicious processes and remove unauthorized scheduled tasks or backdoor accounts.
- Perform a full antivirus/EDR scan on the compromised machines before restoring access.

5. COMMUNICATIONS:
- Notify the IT security manager and the affected business unit stakeholder with an impact summary.
